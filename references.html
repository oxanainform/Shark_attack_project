03 Why focus on recent years?

Participation changes the picture

The estimated share of women among surfers worldwide rose from 25% in 2010 to about 38% in 2023. That growth is one reason we focus our main comparison on 2000 onward: older attack records reflect different participation patterns and are not a like-for-like measure of today’s exposure.

Earlier records still provide useful historical context. Surfing estimates also describe surfers, not every water activity or every country, so they cannot by themselves explain differences in shark-attack counts.
